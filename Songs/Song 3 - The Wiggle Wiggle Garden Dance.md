## Tips for Suno
- The **FREEZE** moments work best when the music stops for a beat. If Suno keeps the music playing, that's okay: I'll freeze the picture in the video, and kids will still get the game.
- Make 4–6 versions and pick the one where "FREEZE!" is the clearest and the energy stays high.
