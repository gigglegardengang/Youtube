# Song 4: The Garden ABC Song

**Teaches:** the alphabet A to Z, plus the sound each letter makes
**Lead:** Lulu, inching along a path of giant letter stepping-stones, with a cameo from each friend on their letter (B for Bo, P for Poppy, S for Sunny, Z for Zoom)
**Where:** Garden Wide (the letter path) and Leaf Bed
**Length:** about 2:45

## Suno settings
- **Mode:** Custom
- **Persona / Voice:** Giggle Garden Singer
- **Title:** The Garden ABC Song
- **Style:**

```text
Upbeat preschool children's pop, gentle bouncy inching rhythm, 112 BPM, major key, ukulele, xylophone, glockenspiel, soft hand claps, light acoustic drums, warm friendly female lead vocal, very clear slow diction for letter sounds, happy kids choir echoing the letters, call and response, sing-along alphabet phonics song, playful, catchy
```

- **Exclude styles:** `sad, minor key, heavy bass, autotune, rap, distorted, too fast`

## Tips for Suno
- The letter sounds are spelled the way they sound ("buh, buh", "sss, sss") so Suno says the sound, not the letter name. Make 4–6 versions and pick the one where the sounds come through clearest.
- The bridge is spoken or chanted, not sung to the "Twinkle Twinkle" tune, so it stays this song's own.
- If Suno rushes the verses, add `slow and clear` to the start of the style.

## On-screen graphics (free, no tokens)
For each letter: a giant letter stone, the word, and a picture or emoji (🍎 🐝 🐛 🌼 🪱 🌸 🌱 🍯 🐞 🦘 🪁 🐛 🍄 🪺 🍊 🐞 🤫 🌈 🌻 🌷 ☂️ 💜 〰️ 🎶 🥱 🐌). Letters that don't need their own clip (E, G, I, N, O, V, X) use graphics over a letter-path clip.
