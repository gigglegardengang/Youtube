# Song 2: Poppy's Counting Spots

**Teaches:** counting 1 to 10, plus counting Poppy's 7 spots
**Where:** the Puddle Pond, where Poppy hops across the lily pads
**Length:** about 2 minutes

## Suno settings
- **Mode:** Custom
- **Persona / Voice:** Giggle Garden Singer
- **Title:** Poppy's Counting Spots
- **Style:**

```text
Upbeat preschool children's pop, bouncy hopping rhythm, 120 BPM, major key, ukulele, xylophone, glockenspiel, hand claps, light acoustic drums, little water splash sound effects, warm friendly female lead vocal, clear simple diction, happy kids choir shouting the numbers, call and response, sing-along counting song, playful, catchy
```

- **Exclude styles:** `sad, minor key, heavy bass, autotune, rap, distorted`

## Lyrics

```text
[Intro]
[Spoken, cheerful]
Hi, friends! It's me, Poppy! Let's count together!
[Kids Choir]
Yaaay!

[Chorus]

Hop along and count with me!

[Chorus]
Count with Poppy, one, two, three!

Let's count again, nice and loud!
```
