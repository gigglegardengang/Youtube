# Welcome to the Giggle Garden: Pixar-Style Shot List

**Song:** Welcome to the Giggle Garden.mp3 (2:15, 115 BPM)
**New clips to make:** 21 (Clips 02–22; Clip 01 is already done)
**Rendar settings for every clip:** Seedance 1.5 Pro · 16:9 · 720p · 5 sec · Audio Off · Private (110 tokens each)
**Estimated cost:** about 2,300 tokens, or about 2,700 with retries

⭐ = **library clip**, reused in future songs (opening, ending, chorus dances and signature moves)

## How each shot is made
1. **ChatGPT scene picture:** upload the listed files, then paste the scene prompt followed by the style lock below. Save it as `Scenes/Shot NN ....png`.
2. **Rendar clip:** upload the scene picture as the **Start frame**, then paste the motion prompt followed by the motion lock below. Save it as `Videos/Clips/Clip NN ....mp4`.

**Style lock** (add to the end of every scene prompt):
> Keep every character exactly the same as their reference sheet: same design, colors, face, eyes and proportions. Light the characters with the same warm golden sunlight as the background, with soft contact shadows under them so they truly stand in the scene. Pixar-style 3D render, wide 16:9 landscape image, no text, no extra characters.

**Motion lock** (add to the end of every motion prompt):
> Keep every character's design exactly the same. Smooth, bouncy Pixar-style 3D animation, gentle natural motion, flowers swaying softly. No new characters, no text.

**Reference sheets:** Poppy = `Characters/Poppy/Adorable Ladybug Character Turnaround Sheet.png` · Sunny = `Characters/Sunny/Sunny Reference Sheet.png` · Bo = `Characters/Bo/Bo Character Sheet.png` · Zoom = `Characters/Zoom/Zoom Character Sheet.png` · Lulu = `Characters/Lulu/Lulu Character Sheet.png`
