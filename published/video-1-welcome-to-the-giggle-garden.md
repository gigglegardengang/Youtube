## 📝 Description (copy/paste)
🌻 Come on down to the Giggle Garden! 🌻

Meet Sunny the sunflower, Poppy the curious ladybug, Bo the brave bumblebee, Zoom the (not-so-fast) snail, and Lulu the snack-loving caterpillar in this happy sing-along theme song for kids!

Your little one will:
🐞 Count Poppy's spots from 1 to 7
🐝 Take a calm, deep breath with Bo
🐌 Giggle at Zoom's super-slow race
🐛 Dance along: jump, buzz, wiggle, sway and zoom!

Perfect for toddlers and preschoolers ages 3–6. Sing it, dance it, and come grow with us! 🌱

✨ New songs every week! Subscribe to Giggle Garden Gang so you never miss a song.

🎵 LYRICS
Good morning, Giggle Garden!
Come on down to Giggle Garden, where the flowers sway and the sunbeams shine!
Wiggle your toes and giggle a little, everybody's welcome, every time!
Giggle, giggle, Giggle Garden, come and grow with me!

Here's Sunny, tall and yellow, stretching up to say hello!
She wakes up with the morning sun, and waves her leaves at everyone!

Here comes Poppy, red and spotty, asking "Why?" to everybody!
Count her spots: one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, whee!

Buzz, buzz, here comes Bo, fuzzy, fuzzy, round and slow!
When he's scared, he takes a breath. Breathe in, breathe out, he does his best!

Look out, look out, it's Zoom the snail, the fastest snail on the garden trail!
Ready, set, here I gooo! Oh no, Zoom, you're still so slow!

Munch, munch, Lulu's here, the sleepiest caterpillar of the year!
Apples, berries, peas, a treat. Snack time, snack time, yum, let's eat!

Now jump like a ladybug! Buzz like a bumblebee! Wiggle like a caterpillar! Sway like a sunflower! And zoom like a snail, nice and slow…

See you in the garden! Bye-bye! 👋
