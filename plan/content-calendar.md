## 📅 60-Day Plan: How this calendar works

## The weekly rhythm (after launch)
- **Monday: 🎓 Learning song.** Letters, numbers, colors, shapes, opposites, days of the week
- **Wednesday: 💛 Habits & feelings song.** Brushing teeth, sharing, manners, big feelings, being brave
- **Friday: 🎉 Fun Friday.** Alternates between a new dance or bedtime song and a **compilation** (no new tokens)

That's 24 videos over 8 weeks, plus the 3 launch videos, for **27 videos by Day 60**. Then drop to 1 a week.

## Each card has
Lead character · where it happens · what kids learn · the song hook · which clips we can reuse · estimated Rendar tokens

## Rendar token budget
- New song: about **600–900 tokens** (6–8 new clips at 110 each; the rest reused)
- Compilation: **0 tokens**
- 20 new songs × ~700 ≈ **14,000 tokens over 2 months**. You have ~5,300 now + 6,000/month on Creator ≈ 17,300. ✅ Fits, with a little room for redos.

## How to make each one (same as Songs 2 & 3)
1. Tell Claude "let's do [card name]". Claude writes the lyrics + Suno prompt (saved in **Song Lyrics**).
2. You make the song in Suno with the Giggle Garden Singer and save the MP3 in `Giggle Garden/Songs/`.
3. Claude makes the scenes and clips in Chrome, cuts the video, makes the thumbnail and Shorts, and adds the upload card to **Ready to Upload**.
4. Move this card to **Video Published** when it's live.

## Batch tip
Make 2–3 songs in one Suno session so you're always a week ahead.

## 🦋 Saved for a milestone
**"Lulu Becomes a Butterfly":** save it for 1,000 subscribers or video #50. It's a big emotional moment kids will love.

## W1 · Mon · 🔤 The Garden ABC Song (Lulu)

**Type:** 🎓 Learning · **Lead:** Lulu 🐛 · **Where:** Leaf Bed + Garden Wide
**Kids learn:** the alphabet A–Z, letter sounds
**Hook:** Lulu inches along a path of giant letter stepping-stones, and each letter matches a garden thing: "A is for Apple 🍎, B is for Bo 🐝, C is for Caterpillar 🐛…" Chorus: "A-B-C, come and sing with me!"
**Why it works:** "ABC song" is one of the most searched kids' topics on YouTube.
**Reuse:** Lulu clips (16, 17, 20, L1), cameos from every character for B (Bo), P (Poppy), S (Sunny), Z (Zoom)
**New clips:** about 7 (Lulu inching on letter stones, apple, rain, rainbow…). Letters are free on-screen graphics.
**Tokens:** ~770

## W1 · Wed · 🪥 Brush, Brush, Brush with Bo

**Type:** 💛 Habit · **Lead:** Bo 🐝 · **Where:** Puddle Pond (sink-like water)
**Kids learn:** brushing teeth for 2 minutes: top, bottom, front, back, then spit and rinse
**Hook:** Bo doesn't want to brush... until his friends make it a dance. "Brush, brush, brush, scrub-a-dub-dub, top and bottom, round we rub!" The song lasts about 2 minutes on purpose, so kids can brush along to it.
**Why it works:** Parents search "brush teeth song" every night. It gets replayed again and again.
**Reuse:** Bo clips (12, 13, 19, B1), the group cheer (08)
**New clips:** about 6 (Bo with a tiny toothbrush brushing up/down/sides, foamy giggle, sparkly smile, rinse)
**Tokens:** ~660
